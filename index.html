About

I'm a frontend engineer in Campo Grande, Brazil, and for the past ten years I've worked on eSaniagro, the system Mato Grosso do Sul uses to manage animal and plant health. More than 90,000 people use it, from office staff to inspectors out in the field.

Those inspectors are the reason I care so much about offline-first. They work in rural areas where there's usually no signal, so the React Native apps I built keep everything on the device and sync when they're back in range. Moving that work off paper took inspection processing from up to seven days down to one.

Most of my day is React, React Native and TypeScript, but I also work on the .NET APIs and SQL Server databases behind them. Knowing how the data actually behaves makes the interface a lot easier to get right.
